# Source and maintenance

Chen Jiang completed this individual Dartmouth ENGS108 project in Fall 2022.
`historical/student_cells.py` preserves selected student implementations as
notebook fragments. The runnable modules and demonstration include 2026
AI-assisted repairs and independently implemented supporting code.

The vision workflow uses scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras and the upstream Xception base.
Student-written models and transfer heads are identified in the README.
